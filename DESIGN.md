# Visual Authority & Design System

## Theme: Academic Heritage & Editorial Ink
Derived from the project reference showcase (`showcase/index.html`) for Xavier AlumniConnect.

### Core Palette & Tokens
- **Canvas / Backgrounds:**
  - `cream`: `#f4efe6` (Main canvas background)
  - `cream-dark`: `#e8dfd0` (Card wells, subtle section backgrounds)
  - `cream-deeper`: `#d9ccba` (Heavy borders, inset accents)
- **Ink / Typography:**
  - `ink-900` / `ink`: `#1a1410` (Primary high-contrast text, dark surfaces)
  - `ink-700` / `ink-2`: `#3d3222` (Secondary headings, dark borders)
  - `ink-600` / `ink-3`: `#5c4d37` (Muted body copy, captions, timestamps)
  - `ink-500`: `#7d6a4f` (Placeholders, secondary icon fills)
- **Brand & Actions:**
  - `navy`: `#152744` (Primary button background, active nav highlights, badges)
  - `navy-light` / `navy-l`: `#1e3a6e` (Hover states for primary buttons)
  - `navy-pale`: `#dde8f7` (Info banners, focus halos)
- **Warm Accent:**
  - `amber`: `#c4821a` (Warm gold highlight, status badges, progress bars)
  - `amber-light` / `amber-l`: `#e8a93c` (Hover states, glowing points)
  - `amber-pale`: `#fdf3e3` (Pending review alerts, warm highlight containers)
- **Verified & Success Status:**
  - `moss`: `#3a5c3e` (Verified alumni badges, live status stamps, success notifications)
  - `moss-light`: `#4e7a53` (Success buttons, hover rings)
- **Dividers & Rules:**
  - `rule`: `rgba(26, 20, 16, 0.12)` (Thin 1px ink rule borders)

### Typography
- **Headlines / Display:** `DM Serif Display`, `Georgia`, serif
- **Body & Controls:** `DM Sans`, `Inter`, sans-serif
- **Data, Badges & Code:** `IBM Plex Mono`, monospace
